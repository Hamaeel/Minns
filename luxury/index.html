Introduction

Minns Water Sports (MWS Boats) is a self-drive boat rental company in George Town, Great Exuma. It rents 17' to 26' Boston Whalers and Power Cats for 6 to 10 people, for day trips inside Elizabeth Harbour. No boating licence is needed, and drivers must be 18 or older.

Do I need a boat licence?

No. In the Bahamas you do not need a licence. The driver just has to be 18 or older.

Which boat should I pick?

Pick by how many people are coming: the 17' and 19' boats fit 6, the 20' fits 7, the 21' and 22' fit 8, and the 26' fits 10.

How much does it cost?

From $316 to $607 a day. Rent for more days and each day costs less. You also pay for the fuel you use, plus 12% VAT (no VAT on the deposit).

Do I get my deposit back?

Yes. When you bring the boat back, we fill the tank, take the fuel cost from your deposit and give you the rest back.

Where can I go?

Anywhere inside Elizabeth Harbour, about 7 by 1.5 miles, from Lilly Cay to Man O War Cay. To go further, book a local charter.
