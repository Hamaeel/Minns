How it works

4 easy steps

Getting your boat is quick and easy.

  1. 1. Book

    Send us your dates. A card deposit holds your boat.

  2. 2. Come to our office

    Sign the papers in George Town and pay.

  3. 3. Have fun

    Take the boat out and explore the harbour.

  4. 4. Back by 5 PM

    We fill the tank and give back the rest of your deposit.

Need to cancel? Tell us 3 days before. There is a 15% fee, and if you do not tell us you lose the deposit. Bad weather? You can move or cancel your day.

Do I need a boat licence?

No. In the Bahamas you do not need a licence. The driver just has to be 18 or older.

Which boat should I pick?

Pick by how many people are coming: the 17' and 19' boats fit 6, the 20' fits 7, the 21' and 22' fit 8, and the 26' fits 10.

How much does it cost?

From $316 to $607 a day. Rent for more days and each day costs less. You also pay for the fuel you use, plus 12% VAT (no VAT on the deposit).

Do I get my deposit back?

Yes. When you bring the boat back, we fill the tank, take the fuel cost from your deposit and give you the rest back.

Where can I go?

Anywhere inside Elizabeth Harbour, about 7 by 1.5 miles, from Lilly Cay to Man O War Cay. To go further, book a local charter.

Do I bring the boat back every day?

Yes. Every boat must be back at our dock by 5 PM each day, even on longer rentals.

What if the weather is bad?

You can move your rental to another day or cancel it before you go out.

Can you bring the boat to my hotel?

No. Come to our office in George Town to sign the papers and pick up your boat.
